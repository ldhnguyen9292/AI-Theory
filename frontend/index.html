My Background

Bridging Medicine & Tech

A Unique Technical Perspective

Before shifting full-time into software engineering, I dedicated 6 years to studying medicine, graduating as a General Practitioner (Medical Doctor), and spent nearly 4 years working as a Health Quarantine Officer at the Ho Chi Minh City Center for Disease Control (CDC).

During the height of the COVID-19 pandemic, I realized the enormous impact of software when I successfully built and automated passenger epidemiological tracking systems using Excel, VBA, and Selenium. This first-hand experience of solving critical real-world crises through code inspired my transition into full-stack web development.

Today, I combine my strict clinical training—which taught me critical problem-solving, absolute attention to detail, and calm decision-making under high pressure—with modern backend and frontend software engineering practices to write reliable, highly-scalable software.
